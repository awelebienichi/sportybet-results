Daily rollover

One safe ticket a day of about your target odds, built with the strictest checks on the builder: BOTH teams 85%+ for the pick in their own last 10, plus head-to-head (75%+), Flashscore home/away form (80%+) or footybets agreeing, and nothing against it. A win rolls the whole return into tomorrow's stake; a loss starts again from your starting stake. Each day's code arrives as a notification at the time you set.
